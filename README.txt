THE BIGGER STORY HUB — WEBSITE PACKAGE

CONTENTS
- index.html: responsive, static website homepage.

DEPLOYMENT
1. Open your Netlify dashboard.
2. Open the THE BIGGER STORY HUB site.
3. Go to Deploys.
4. Drag the extracted folder (or its contents, with index.html at the top level) into the manual deploy area.
5. Wait for the deploy to finish, then refresh your live site.

IMPORTANT EDITORIAL NOTES
- Story cards are sample placeholder content, not verified news. Replace them with original, fact-checked reporting before promoting them as live news.
- Search and category filters work in the browser.
- The newsletter form is a visual front-end preview and does not collect or store email addresses. Connect a mailing-list provider or secure backend before collecting subscriptions.
- The site is static HTML and can be hosted on Netlify's free tier.
